Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short: Note Note does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you create stays on your Mac.

Who we are

Note Note is a note-taking app for macOS developed by Alperen Kolaylı (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Note Note has no user accounts and does not send your notes, files, usage data, device identifiers or any other information to us or to anyone else. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party data-collection tools.

Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app saves what you create locally on your Mac, inside the app’s private storage area managed by macOS:

  • Boards, notes, Quick Notes, drawings, checklists and app settings.
  • Files you attach to notes. Attached files are copied into the app’s own folder so they keep working even if the original is moved.
  • Daily backup copies of your boards (the last 14), and boards you deleted during the last 30 days, so they can be restored.
  • If “Show notes in Spotlight” is on (Settings → Data), note titles and a short excerpt are added to macOS Spotlight’s search index on your Mac so you can find them in system search. You can turn this off at any time, which removes them from the index.

This data never leaves your Mac through Note Note. It is included in your normal Mac backups (for example Time Machine) if you use them.

Files you choose to export or import

When you export a board, a note or a PDF/Word/text file, Note Note writes it only to the location you choose in the save dialog. When you import or attach a file, the app reads only the file you select. What happens to exported files afterwards is up to you.

Links and web content

Note Note does not connect to the internet on its own. If you click a link in a note, it opens in your default web browser, and that website’s own privacy policy applies. If you paste content from the web that refers to online images, your Mac may load those images from the original website when the note is shown.

Deleting your data

You can delete any board, note or attachment inside the app at any time. Deleted boards stay in “Recently deleted” for 30 days unless you delete them permanently there. Deleting the Note Note app from your Mac removes all of its stored data. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Note Note is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — we will update this page and the “Effective” date above before the change is released.
