November Novel Board · 50K in 30 days

A free Note Note board for writing 50,000 words between 1 and 30 November

Fifty thousand words in thirty days is about 1,667 words a day. This board gives you a place to plan the book in an evening and a scoreboard to keep you going: fill in your premise and characters, sketch a seven-beat plot, and tick off every day as you write.

Download the board (November_Novel_50K.nnn)Note Note board · about 60 KB

How to open it

  1. Get Note Note on the Mac App Store (free for a limited time).
  2. Download the file with the button above.
  3. Double-click it, or choose Import in Note Note and pick the file.

How to use it

  • Before 1 November: fill in Premise, Characters and World & Setting. Twenty minutes each is enough.
  • Plot: seven cards (hook, inciting incident, first plot point, midpoint, crisis, climax, resolution), each with a short explanation and a “My version” line.
  • Scene list: four weekly cards with word goals and five scene slots each.
  • Daily tracker: 30 checkboxes, from “Day 1 · 1,667 words” to “Day 30 · total 50,000”. Tick a day when you reach its total.
  • Rules for November and Stuck? Prompts: eight rules and ten tiny writing prompts for bad days.
  • After November: what to do on 1 December, from resting to revising.
  • Click a card and press the full-editor button to type straight into the empty lines. Every card is yours to edit.
